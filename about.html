What is Esports?

Esports, or Electronic Sports, is the competitive field for online video games.

Esports has gained popularity with the continued advancement of video games and digital technology. As technology grows, Esports plans to be be seen as a equal sport to other nationally acknowledged sports.

Our Mission

We strive to create a welcoming environment to all students who have a passion for esports. With a strong community, we will offer the best resources we can to our students to be able to complete both nationally and locally at gaming tournaments.

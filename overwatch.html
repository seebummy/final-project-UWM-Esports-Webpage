Overwatch

Overwatch is a 4v4 hero shooter that originally launched to the public in 2014. Throughout the decade, the game has had two iterations and been the foundation for esport shooters.

UWM houses teams of 6, with 4 active players per roster and two “subs”- these are players that can be swapped in when a main team member is unable to play. We have two teams per divisions- JV and Varsity. Teams are larger this year due to a popularity increase.
